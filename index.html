Weekly bonus days

Every 7-day week always has 1 Roles Day (this role's own turn) and 1 different Jack of All Trades Day (any role counts) — 2 boosted days out of 7, giving +25% points that day. They're not optional, so there's no checkbox: the totals below already assume 5 normal days + these 2 bonus days every week.
